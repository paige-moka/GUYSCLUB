Privacy

What you tell us stays with us.

Plain words on what this website collects and what we do with it. Last updated November 2026.

Who we are

This website is run by GUYS&CO MKTG Inc. ("GUYS&CO"), based in Alberta, Canada. Questions about privacy go to {{EMAIL}}.

What we collect

  • Membership applications. Your name, email, business and role, industry and what you write about the value you bring and what you are looking for. Phone and sponsor are optional.
  • I know a guy recommendations. Your name and email, the name of the person you recommend, what you tell us about them and, if you choose to add it, how to reach them. Please only recommend someone who would welcome the introduction.
  • Notes from the contact page. Your name, email, the note you write and, if you choose to add them, your business and phone number.
  • Strategy Call requests. The Book a Strategy Call button opens a Google Form. What you enter there is collected through Google Forms and shared with us.
  • Payments. If you pay through a payment link, the payment happens on a page hosted by Stripe. GUYS&CO never sees or stores your card details. Stripe shares your name, email, the amount and the payment status with us.
  • Member Lounge. If you are a member, the Lounge holds your account and the plans, files and records shared with you there.
  • Site statistics. If analytics are switched on, we see general statistics such as pages viewed and the kind of device used. We don't use them to identify you.

How we use it

We use your information to reply to you, deliver the work you ask for, send receipts and keep our records. We don't sell personal information, and we don't add you to a mailing list unless you ask.

Who helps us

We rely on service providers to run this site: our website host (Vercel), our database provider (Supabase, which stores form submissions and the Member Lounge), our email provider (which delivers form notifications to our inbox), Google (Forms), Stripe (payments) and, if switched on, an analytics provider. Some of them may store information outside Canada.

How long we keep it

We keep applications, recommendations and notes for as long as needed to respond and for our business records, then delete them. Client work is covered by its own agreement and NDA.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you. Send us a note or email {{EMAIL}} and we'll respond within thirty days.
