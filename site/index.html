The membership question

What unique value do you bring to this community?

Not everyone needs a corner office or knows thousands of people. Different members bring different strengths. We just ask that you bring something.

Expertise

The knowledge, skills and experience you have, and the problems you can help others solve.

Connections

The industries, networks, customers, suppliers, communities and decision makers you understand.

Opportunities

What you're building, pursuing or looking for, and where working together could create value for both sides.

Come with a why. Contribute your value. Leave with opportunity.

We believe the strongest business relationships are built on mutual value.

Every member brings something different to the table: expertise, experience, relationships, ideas, resources or opportunities.

You don't have to know everyone. You don't have to be an expert in everything. But you should be willing to contribute something, help where you can, and recognize that the value you receive from a community is connected to the value you help create.

We're here to build relationships that move people and businesses forward.

Membership standards

Clear standards. No red tape.

Participation is part of the culture, not a points system. We keep the bar clear, the process short, and the rules the same for everyone.

A good member

  1. 1Has a legitimate professional purpose, and relevant experience, expertise or business activity.
  2. 2Shows professional integrity and respects confidentiality.
  3. 3Understands reciprocity, and is willing to contribute.
  4. 4Wants real relationships, not a list of prospects.
  5. 5Ideally, is recommended by an existing member.

How approval works

Most applications: a short application, agreement to the standards and, where possible, a member sponsor. Approved on the standards, without interviews.

Exceptions only: if something doesn't fit, we take a closer look, in proportion to the situation.

Sponsorship helps, but it never guarantees acceptance. Every applicant meets the same standards.

Conduct policy. Members who repeatedly spam, exploit, mislead or mistreat others lose their membership. That's the one rule we enforce without exception.
